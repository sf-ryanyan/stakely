## 🇺🇸 English

### 🧭 Overview

Stakely is an investor relations portal for private companies that have raised money from friends, family, angels or local investors.

It gives the company a professional public page, an invite-only data room for new investors, a dashboard for existing shareholders, a private board room, and an admin console for the finance team — all in one website, in English and Chinese.

The demo runs on a fictional company, **Hearth & Grain Bakery Café** (3 stores in Austin, raising a $5M Series A), so you can click through every screen with realistic numbers.

### 🎯 The problems it solves

| 😩 The pain today | ✅ How Stakely fixes it |
|---|---|
| The founder answers the same question from 20 investors, one by one | Questions go into one Q&A board. Each answer is shared with everyone, so it is answered once |
| Shareholders feel that once they invested, they heard nothing back | Monthly updates, quarterly reports and live store progress are always there to check |
| Endless meetings just to explain the numbers | A one-sentence summary, a health check and a scorecard explain the business in 30 seconds |
| Files are scattered across WeChat, email and shared drives | One document library. Every file is watermarked with the viewer's name |
| Numbers differ between decks and spreadsheets | Every number comes from one source, with plan vs. actual shown side by side |
| Finance terms (EBITDA, pro-rata, dilution) confuse non-expert investors | Every term has a plain-language explanation with a worked example |
| It's hard to know which prospects are serious | A fundraising pipeline shows who registered, who opened the data room and who committed |
| Posting fundraising terms publicly can create legal risk | Layered access: the public page shows the brand only; terms are visible to invited investors only |
| Nobody knows who actually read the report | Read tracking shows who opened it, with a one-click reminder for the rest |

### 👥 Who it's for

- 🍞 **Multi-location operators** — restaurant groups, cafés, retail, clinics — with 10 to 200 private shareholders
- 🤝 **Community-funded businesses** where investors are friends, partners and loyal customers
- 💼 **Founders preparing a raise** who want to look as organized as a venture-backed company
- 🧾 **CPAs and advisors** who want to offer their clients a better way to report to shareholders

### 🧩 What's inside — five views of one portal

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| 🌐 **Visitor** | Brand story, stores, team, news, FAQ and an "I'm interested" form. No investment terms |
| 🔑 **Prospective investor** | One-page investment summary, round terms, online deck, financials, key risks, store map, Q&A, book a call, non-binding indication of interest |
| 📈 **Shareholder** | My investment, company health, scorecard, interactive trends, return simulator, reports, announcements, Q&A, documents, meetings & online voting |
| ⚖️ **Board director** | Everything a shareholder sees, plus a confidential board room: board pack, minutes, resolutions to sign |
| 🛠️ **Administrator** | Shareholder registry, funding history, fundraising pipeline, publish updates, Q&A inbox, document permissions, read tracking |

### ✨ Highlights

- 🧮 **Return simulator** — Drag sliders for store count, revenue per store and sale value, and switch Series A participation on or off. The shareholder instantly sees what their stake could be worth in 2029, including how the liquidation preference works in a low-value sale.
- 🗺️ **Store map** — Open stores, the store under construction, signed leases and target neighborhoods. Tap any point for photos and numbers.
- 💵 **"Where every $1 goes"** — A waterfall chart from $1.00 of sales down to 7¢ of operating profit.
- 📋 **Quarterly scorecard** — Eight metrics, each compared with last quarter and plan, with a status and a reason for every miss.
- 📊 **Interactive trends** — Switch between revenue, customers per day, average ticket and members, over 6 months, year to date or 12 months.
- 🔥 **Customer heatmap** — When customers come in, by day and hour.
- ✅ **"Needs your attention"** — Votes, RSVPs, allocations and unread reports, each with a deadline.
- 🔔 **"Since your last visit"** — What changed, so every login has something new.
- 🌏 **English / 中文** — Switch language on any page. Works on desktop and mobile.

### 🕹️ How to try it

1. Open the [live demo](https://sf-ryanyan.github.io/stakely/).
2. Click **Investor sign in** and choose a role. No password needed.
3. Try these five things:
   - 🌐 As a **Visitor**, register interest at the bottom of the page → switch to **Administrator** → open **Series A pipeline**. Your entry is there as a new website lead.
   - 📈 As a **Shareholder**, open **Return simulator** and press **Conservative**, **Base plan** and **Upside**.
   - 💬 As a **Shareholder**, ask a question → switch to **Administrator** → answer it in the **Q&A inbox** → switch back to see the answer.
   - 📣 As an **Administrator**, publish an update → switch to **Shareholder** and find it in **Announcements**.
   - ⚖️ As a **Board director**, open **Board Room** and sign the pending resolution.
4. Use **Reset demo data** on the sign-in page to start over.

### 🔧 Adapting it for a real company

The demo is a template. For a real company it can be rebranded with the company's own name, colors and photos. Numbers can sync from the company's POS and accounting systems. Each person gets their own secure login with two-factor sign-in, and access can be set file by file and role by role.

### ⚠️ Disclaimer

Hearth & Grain Bakery Café is a fictional company. All names, numbers and documents are made up for demonstration. Nothing in this project is an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy securities. The return simulator is an illustration, not a forecast or promise. In the demo, data you enter stays in your own browser only.

## 🇨🇳 中文

### 🧭 项目简介

Stakely 是一个为私营公司打造的**投资者关系门户**，适合向亲友、天使投资人或本地投资人融过资的公司。

它在一个网站里提供：专业的公司公开页、给新投资人的受邀资料室、给现有股东的看板、保密的董事会专区，以及给财务团队的管理后台。全站中英双语。

演示使用一家虚构公司 **Hearth & Grain Bakery Café**（奥斯汀 3 家门店，正在进行 500 万美元 A 轮融资），每个页面都可以用接近真实的数据点开体验。

### 🎯 解决什么问题

| 😩 现在的痛点 | ✅ Stakely 怎么解决 |
|---|---|
| 同一个问题，创始人要一个个回答 20 个投资人 | 所有问题进入统一问答区，回答一次，所有人可见 |
| 股东觉得钱投进去就没消息了 | 月度简报、季度报告、门店进展随时可查 |
| 为了解释数字，会议开不完 | 一句话总结、健康度和记分卡，30 秒看懂公司现状 |
| 文件散落在微信、邮件和网盘里 | 统一文件库，每份文件自动带查看人姓名水印 |
| 演示文稿和表格里的数字前后不一致 | 所有数字只有一个来源，计划和实际并排对比 |
| EBITDA、按比例认购、稀释这些词，普通投资人看不懂 | 每个术语都有白话解释和计算实例 |
| 不知道哪些意向投资人是认真的 | 融资漏斗显示谁登记了、谁看了资料室、谁做了承诺 |
| 公开宣传融资条款有法律风险 | 分层权限：公开页只展示品牌，条款只给受邀投资人看 |
| 不知道到底谁看了报告 | 阅读追踪显示谁打开了报告，一键提醒没看的人 |

### 👥 适合谁

- 🍞 **多门店经营者**：餐饮集团、咖啡店、零售、诊所等，拥有 10 到 200 位私人股东
- 🤝 **社区集资型企业**：投资人是朋友、合作伙伴和老客户
- 💼 **准备融资的创始人**：希望和有风投背景的公司一样专业、有条理
- 🧾 **会计师与顾问**：想为客户提供更好的股东汇报方式

### 🧩 包含内容：同一门户的五种视角

| 身份 | 能看到什么 |
|---|---|
| 🌐 **访客** | 品牌故事、门店、团队、动态、FAQ、"我有兴趣"登记表，不展示任何融资条款 |
| 🔑 **意向投资人** | 一页看懂的投资摘要、本轮条款、在线演示文稿、财务、主要风险、门店地图、问答、预约通话、不具约束力的意向登记 |
| 📈 **股东** | 我的投资、公司健康度、记分卡、可交互趋势图、回报模拟器、报告、公告、问答、文件、股东会与在线投票 |
| ⚖️ **董事** | 股东可见的全部内容，外加保密的董事会专区：资料包、会议纪要、待签署决议 |
| 🛠️ **管理员** | 股东名册、融资历史、融资漏斗、发布更新、问答收件箱、文件权限、阅读追踪 |

### ✨ 亮点功能

- 🧮 **回报模拟器**：拖动门店数、单店营收、出售估值三个滑块，并选择是否参与 A 轮认购，股东立刻看到 2029 年自己的股份可能值多少，还能直观理解低估值出售时清算优先权的影响。
- 🗺️ **门店地图**：已开业门店、在建门店、已签租约和目标社区一目了然，点任意位置查看照片和数据。
- 💵 **"每 1 美元去哪了"**：瀑布图展示每 1 美元销售额如何一步步变成 7 美分经营利润。
- 📋 **季度记分卡**：8 项指标对比上季度和计划，每项都有状态，没达标的写明原因。
- 📊 **可交互趋势图**：营收、日均客流、客单价、会员数随意切换，时间可选 6 个月、年初至今或 12 个月。
- 🔥 **客流热力图**：按星期和小时显示顾客什么时候来。
- ✅ **"待你处理"**：投票、出席回复、认购额度、未读报告，每项都有截止日期。
- 🔔 **"自上次登录以来"**：显示有什么变化，让每次登录都有新内容。
- 🌏 **中英双语**：任何页面一键切换，电脑和手机都能用。

### 🕹️ 怎么体验

1. 打开 [在线演示](https://sf-ryanyan.github.io/stakely/)。
2. 点击 **Investor sign in（投资人登录）**，选择一个身份，不需要密码。
3. 推荐试试这五件事：
   - 🌐 以**访客**身份在页面底部登记意向 → 切换到**管理员** → 打开 **A 轮意向漏斗**，你的登记会作为新的网站线索出现。
   - 📈 以**股东**身份打开**回报模拟器**，分别点击**保守**、**基准计划**、**乐观**。
   - 💬 以**股东**身份提一个问题 → 切换到**管理员**，在**问答收件箱**里回答 → 再切回股东查看回答。
   - 📣 以**管理员**身份发布一条更新 → 切换到**股东**，在**公告**里找到它。
   - ⚖️ 以**董事**身份进入**董事会专区**，签署待签的决议。
4. 想从头开始，在登录页点击 **Reset demo data（重置演示数据）**。

### 🔧 用于真实公司

这个演示是一套模板。用于真实公司时，可以换成公司自己的名称、配色和照片；数字可以从公司的 POS 和会计系统自动同步；每个人用自己的安全账号登录并开启双重验证；文件权限可以按文件、按身份单独设置。
